---
title: Slack Annoyances
description: recommended programs
layout: post
---

- Encourages slapdash, shallow communication, but gives every message a permalink.
- Enables animated emoji by default.
- Only allows selecting three quick emoji.
- "Markdown" syntax is different from everybody else's, even for basics like boldface.
- Keyboard shortcuts for WYSIWG editing differ from the norm, e.g. Command+Shift+U rather than Command+K for hyperlinking.
- Can't require replies in a thread.
- Can't configure a channel to prevent forwarding of messages
- No facility for setting a title or other label for posts or threads, apart from starting them with bold test.
- Pushes unwelcome AI features.
