I can heartily recommend that anyone following this blog for things open source, especially around funding or "sustainability", read Laurie Voss' ["Nobody pays for open source. We can force them to."](https://seldo.com/posts/nobody-pays-for-open-source-we-can-force-them-to).

It's fair to say I'm biased, a bit of a seldo fan.  I was his "battle lawyer" for npm, which he co-founded and I represented for several years.  I admire his frank assessment of some of that experience in-post, but there's lots more there to think about, besides.
